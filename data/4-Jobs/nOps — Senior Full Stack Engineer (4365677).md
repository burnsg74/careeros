**About nOps**

nOps is the leading automated FinOps platform for cloud cost optimization, managing over $5B in annual cloud spend for companies from fast growing startups to global enterprises across AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud. The team is looking for a senior full stack engineer to take ownership of infrastructure, APIs, and platform development end to end.

**The Role**
- Owns full project delivery from architecture through production, across backend infrastructure, APIs, lakehouse connections, and front end work.
- Builds the customer ingestion and authentication flows that power the nOps platform.
- Works hands on across the stack, with a strong backend and infrastructure focus.
- Mentors junior developers and helps shape engineering best practices as the team scales.

**Responsibilities**

Infrastructure and Architecture:

- Designs, builds, and maintains systems on AWS using infrastructure as code.
- Owns systems architecture decisions and ensures scalability, security, and reliability across the platform.
- Modernizes fragmented and legacy processes so the team can move faster.

Platform Development:

- Builds and ships new features end to end, from backend services and APIs to front end interfaces.
- Develops customer integrations and data ingestion flows that connect into Databricks and our lakehouse.
- Collaborates with product managers to scope, estimate, and deliver projects on tight timelines.

Engineering Excellence:

- Writes clean, well tested, maintainable code in Python, JavaScript, and SQL.
- Reviews code, provides feedback, and supports junior engineers as they grow.
- Uses AI coding tools like Cursor and Claude Code as a core part of the development workflow.

**Requirements**
- 4 plus years of experience as a software engineer, ideally with a senior title at a larger company.
- Deep AWS infrastructure experience is a must, including strong fluency in systems architecture and infrastructure as code.
- Strong proficiency in Python, JavaScript, and SQL.
- Experience taking projects from blank page to production with minimal hand holding.
- Demonstrated comfort working across the stack, with a particular strength on the backend.
- Active use of AI coding tools like Cursor or Claude Code in your day to day workflow.
- Self directed operator who thrives in a fast moving, build it yourself environment.

**Nice to Have**
- Experience with Databricks, Vercel, or WorkOS.
- Exposure to GCP or Azure in addition to AWS.
- Background in FinOps, cloud cost optimization, observability, or a related infrastructure or DevOps category.
- Experience mentoring junior developers or leading small project teams.
- Familiarity with Postgres or other relational databases.

**Why nOps**
- Senior level seat with real ownership over infrastructure and platform decisions from day one.
- Direct line to engineering leadership and executive backing to scale the platform as the company grows.
- Real impact on a fast growing FinOps platform powering enterprise cloud cost optimization across AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud.
