**About Flycore**

Flycore is building the operating system for flight schools.

We help aviation businesses manage inquiries, enrollment, communication, and operations through a modern CRM and workflow platform designed specifically for flight training.

We are backed by strong early customers, aviation operators, and investors. We’re growing quickly and looking for a high-agency engineer to help us scale from strong traction to category leader.

**The Role**

We’re hiring a full-time software engineer to work directly with the founders to expand and scale Flycore.

This is not a narrow implementation role. You will:
* Ship features end-to-end
* Improve system architecture as we scale
* Work directly with customers to understand workflows
* Make product and technical tradeoffs
* Help define engineering standards and long-term direction

You’ll have meaningful ownership over both product and technical decisions.

**What You’ll Work On**
* Core CRM workflows and automations
* Messaging systems (email, SMS)
* Usage-based systems (including e-sign capacity and billing logic)
* Internal admin and operational tooling
* Performance and reliability improvements
* AI-assisted workflow enhancements

Our stack includes Next.js, Postgres, Vercel, and modern React component systems. Strong engineering fundamentals matter more than exact stack experience.

**AI-Native Engineering Environment**

We operate in an AI-native development environment.

We expect engineers to:
* Regularly use AI coding assistants such as Cursor, Claude Code, Copilot, or similar
* Leverage LLMs for refactoring, test generation, debugging, and architectural exploration
* Think critically about when AI accelerates development and when human judgment is required

Experience working effectively with AI-assisted IDEs is strongly preferred. We are building an engineering culture that embraces modern tooling to move faster without sacrificing quality.

*We'll ask you in the interview to walk us through a recent project where you used AI tooling in a meaningful way — what you used, what worked, and what you'd do differently.*

**You Might Be a Fit If You**
* Have 5+ years building and shipping production web software
* Have experience building 0 → 1 systems or scaling early-stage products
* Understand database design and system architecture
* Write clean, maintainable, testable code
* Operate independently without heavy oversight
* Are comfortable making pragmatic tradeoffs
* Are already using AI tools as part of your daily development workflow

**Bonus if you:**
* Have vertical SaaS experience
* Have startup experience
* Have built billing or usage-based systems
* Have interest or experience in aviation

**Compensation &amp; Structure**
* Salary: $100,000–$140,000 (based on experience and impact)
* Equity: Meaningful early-stage ownership
* Location: Fully remote (U.S.-based preferred)
* Remote-first team with founders based in Denver and California
