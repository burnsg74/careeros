This role is fully remote, 20-28 hours/week. 

**Responsibilities:**
* Design, build, and maintain both the frontend and backend of a new platforms, from feature development through production.
* Lead and contribute to projects, including new features where the requirements aren't fully defined yet.
* Build and maintain integrations between the platform and its underlying cloud and AI infrastructure.
* Partner with business teams to translate day-to-day workflows into working software
* Implement secure authentication, access control, and data governance appropriate for sensitive business data.
* Contribute to and follow engineering best practices — testing, CI/CD, deployment, and observability.
* Iterate on features based on real usage and feedback, balancing speed with reliability.

**Qualifications:**
* Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems, or a related technical field (or equivalent practical experience).
* 3–5 years of professional software engineering experience, with hands-on exposure to both frontend and backend systems
* Experience creating and maintaining reusable frontend components used across large product surfaces
* Experience building or integrating AI-powered or generative AI tooling to support developer workflows or design systems
* Solid Python skills; experience with FastAPI or a comparable async Python web framework.
* Solid React.js or similar modern frontend app framework
* TypeScript plus Node.js experience - shipped features in a production environment.
* Experience with SQL and NoSQL data stores; data science experience a plus
* Familiarity with LLM/AI API integrations - agentic or tool-calling patterns are a plus.
* Experience with a major cloud provider - comfortable deploying and managing cloud resources.
* Hands-on experience with CI/CD pipelines and containerized deployments.
* Start up or small company experience a must
* Ability to work independently with minimal supervision and feedback
