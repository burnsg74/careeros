### **About Software Velocity Corp**
Software Velocity Corp is an early-stage (pre-seed) startup based in Greater Boston, on a mission to reinvent how the world tests software. Development teams are shipping faster than ever before, especially with AI-accelerated development, yet testing can't keep up with the speed or thoroughness modern software demands. We're building an intelligent platform that closes that gap, giving software teams in any industry, domain, or platform the ability to ship higher-quality software dramatically faster. We're small, focused, and building something we believe will genuinely reshape the industry. And we're looking for a few exceptional people to build it with us. 

### **About the role**
As one of our early full-stack engineers, you'll build across the entire platform: from the interfaces our users live in every day, through the APIs and services behind them, to the data and infrastructure underneath. Working shoulder-to-shoulder with our Chief Architect and core team, you'll help define not just the product, but the architecture, patterns, and quality bar that everything else is built on. This is a genuinely high-ownership role: the services you design, the APIs you shape, and the trade-offs you make will become the backbone of our platform. You'll turn ambiguous, interesting problems into resilient, scalable systems — moving fluidly between layers, choosing the right tool or method at each one, and owning the outcome from commit to production. If you're happiest when there's no part of the stack you can't reach into and improve, this is a rare chance to set the foundation.

Successful candidates will have a strong background in building products or microservices, utilizing cloud services, APIs, and **AI-development assistance** to meet design and business objectives. You will be solving diverse and interesting problems, transforming our vision into reality, while being accountable for the quality, correctness, and resilience of what ships to production.  

### Our Ask of You
Startups are dynamic, fluid, and fast-paced. This is not a role for everyone. We think you'll thrive here if a few things ring true: 
* Our mission genuinely excites you, and you want to help shape it from the ground up. 
* You bring **10+ years of professional software engineering experience**, including hands-on ownership of architecting, building, and evolving production systems.
* You embrace **AI-native development:** you already use AI tools as a core part of how you design, build, and test, and you're excited to push what they make possible.
* You've been **personally accountable for software quality** e.g. test driven development, design for testability, failure prevention, learning from real production issues, etc.
* You do excellent work **semi-autonomously**, with strong technical and quality judgment.
* You're comfortable engaging as an independent contractor, compensated through a combination of cash and scheduled equity under a written contractor agreement with cash compensation in the posted range on an annualized basis.
* You're curious, collaborative, low-ego, and equally eager to learn and to teach.

### Key Responsibilities
* **Build across the stack:** Design and build features end to end, from responsive, high-performance interfaces to the services, APIs, and data layers behind them.
* **Own the architecture:** Establish the front-end patterns, components, and standards the rest of the team will build on.
* **Design for scale:** Partner on end-to-end systems optimized for speed, reliability, and growth.
* **Engineer for quality:** Build with testing and resilience designed in and take pride in what ships.
* **Collaborate closely:** Partner with UX to bring designs to life, shape clean API contracts across the stack, and work with product to turn requirements into features users love.
* **Communicate openly:** Collaborate day to day via Slack, GitHub, and videoconferences.

### Our Culture and who we are: 
We've lived the industry's status quo firsthand and pushed hard against the constraints placed on today's software testers and quality engineers. We believe a genuinely new approach is needed to test modern, complex software at scale — and we're building it. Our culture is curious, low-ego, and high-output: we make fast decisions on reversible calls, protect quality of life as a driver of great work, and celebrate wins together. Most of all, we empower each other to do work that delights our customers and earns their trust.
