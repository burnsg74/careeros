## About Nivelo:

**Everyone deserves to be paid reliably.**

Nivelo’s mission is to provide safe and instantaneous money movement for everyone. Our ambition is large - disrupting a $20 Trillion infrastructure built 50 years ago. Our impact is even bigger - securing the most common and most important payment for most people in the world - their paycheck. We're reinventing the way businesses and individuals move money.

Nivelo is the integrated payments platform that payroll brands use to move money quickly, digitally and securely to guarantee every dollar is delivered on time. 

We were recently featured in [J.P. Morgan's Payments Unbound Magazine](https://www.jpmorgan.com/payments/payments-unbound/magazine/articles/nyc-fintech-world-hotspot) as one of the top five fintechs making waves in NYC's booming payments ecosystem.

The company is backed by investors such as FirstMark Capital, Parameter Ventures, and Anthemis that have taken countless companies to IPO (think Shopify and Airbnb). Investors also include experienced operators in the payment and risk space, including the COO of Stripe, Founder of Tenable, and senior operators at Chime, Stripe, Alloy, and Justworks.

Want to learn more?  Check out this interview on the [Payrollin Podcast](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SjtqRDRAppM).

## **About you:**

You are:

- **A Frontend Expert**: Deep experience with React modern frontend frameworks and familiarity with UI testing tools (bonus if you’ve worked with Next.js and/or Blitz)
- **Product Focused**: You always keep our users’ needs and the usability of our product top of mind.
- **A Proactive Problem Solver**: You don’t wait for instructions—you identify gaps, propose solutions, and take ownership of results.
- **Flexible**: You’re excited to dive in where needed to meet our clients’ needs.
- **A Clear Communicator**: Whether it’s breaking down technical issues for a product manager or communicating incidents during a payroll failure, you keep stakeholders aligned and informed.
- **Operationally Reliable**: You thrive under pressure, stay calm during incidents, and prioritize rapid, effective solutions.

*This is not a job for someone who wants to sit in the backseat. If you’re the kind of engineer who thrives on responsibility, ownership, and building something that matters, we want to hear from you.*

## Requirements

- Experience designing scalable, maintainable and well-tested, high performance frontend architecture
- Experience working on high-stakes, detail-oriented products
- Experience building web applications professionally for 5+ years, with 2+ years of experience in some combination of Typescript/Javascript/React
- Experience with responsible AI usage (both as a feature and for productivity)
- Experience at a small startup (bonus points if it was a fintech startup)
- (bonus) Experience working with SQL/Relational databases



## You’ll Be Working On

- **Frontend Development:**
    - Build fast, user-friendly interfaces to enhance workflows for payroll and treasury professionals.
    - Translate complex data into actionable and clear UI components, ensuring scalability and high responsiveness.
    - Conduct testing and debugging of frontend components to guarantee functionality.
    - Use client insights to build solutions that improve their workflows
    - Develop frontend components that can scale to enterprise-level clients managing millions of payments.
    - Rapidly test and refine features, especially for client-critical workflows like payroll, where delays or errors can have significant downstream effects.
- **Product Collaboration**
    - Break down high-level requests into actionable engineering tasks, estimating complexity and ensuring clarity before diving into development.
    - Collaborate with product team to scope and define key features that address client needs, ensuring alignment with the broader product vision.
