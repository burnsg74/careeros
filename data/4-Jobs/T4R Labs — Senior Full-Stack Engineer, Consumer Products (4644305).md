**About T4R Labs**
T4R Labs is a deep tech lab building at the frontier of AI. We work on hard, unsolved problems where real-time performance, multi-modal understanding, and safety all have to hold up under production load — not just in a demo.

**The Role**
We're looking for a senior full-stack engineer to help turn our realtime, multi-modal AI systems into consumer-grade products people love using. You'll work across the stack — from Django/PostgreSQL services and event-driven architecture on the backend to polished React interfaces on the front — and take features from rough idea to shipped product.

This is a hands-on, high-ownership role on a small team, where your code, your product judgment, and your opinions all matter.

**What You'll Do**
* Design and build consumer-facing features end-to-end, from database schema to UI
* Build and maintain event-driven / event-sourced backend systems in Django
* Ship consumer-grade software — sweat the details that make a product feel finished, not just functional
* Use Claude Code and other AI-assisted development tools as part of your daily workflow to move fast without cutting corners
* Partner closely with the founding team and design to turn rough ideas into shipped features
* Own features from prototype through production, including monitoring and iteration after launch

**What We're Looking For**
* 5–10 years of experience as a full-stack engineer, with real consumer-grade software shipped to real users
* Strong proficiency in Python and React
* Experience with Django, PostgreSQL, and event-driven or event-sourced architectures
* Hands-on, daily experience using Claude Code (or similar AI coding tools)
* Curious, entrepreneurial, and comfortable speaking your mind — we want people who'll push back on a bad idea, ours included
* A self-starter who takes real ownership and accountability, but also works well as part of a small, close-knit team

**Bonus points for:**
* Experience with multi-modal AI
* Experience building products on top of or alongside AI/ML systems
* Experience at an early-stage startup, where the roadmap changes as fast as the product

**Why T4R Labs**
* Work directly with the founding team on problems that don't have off-the-shelf solutions
* High autonomy, high impact — your decisions ship
* We're seed stage: this role is equity only for now, with salary starting once we close funding

**Work Authorization &amp; Background Check**
You must be a U.S. citizen or otherwise authorized to work in the United States without employer sponsorship. As an early-stage company, we're not able to sponsor work visas at this time. Final offers of employment are contingent on successful completion of a background check.

**How to Apply**
Reach out with your resume and a note on the most interesting consumer product you've helped build end-to-end.
