ZukiTalk is an AI voice and text outreach product for automotive dealerships. When a vehicle inspection (MPI) is completed at a dealership, ZukiTalk calls or texts the customer to walk them through the results and collect service approvals without requiring a service advisor to make the call manually. It also sends appointment reminders before a vehicle is scheduled to come in.

The product is a PHP/Symfony web application backed by PostgreSQL. It connects to a small number of external services.

At ZukiTalk, we're changing how dealerships communicate with customers using conversational Voice AI.  We've built a production Voice AI platform that explains vehicle recommendations, answers customer questions, and helps service advisors save time while improving the customer experience.
We're no longer building a prototype. We're in live dealership pilots with enterprise integrations and are looking for an exceptional engineering leader to help us scale.

This is an opportunity to join early, influence the architecture, and help shape the future of the company.
