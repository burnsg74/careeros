**How It All Started**
In 2025, we set out to solve one of healthcare's most human challenges, how to bring empathy back into digital care. As technology reshaped the patient experience, we saw an opportunity to make it more personal, accessible, and compassionate through voice AI.

That's how Pythagoras AI was born. We're building empathetic voice AI agents that helps care teams scale without losing the human connection, bridging the gap between patients and providers in areas like chronic care, and remote assessments. Every interaction matters, and our mission is to make each one feel as real and caring as a human conversation.

**The Team**
We are a team of engineers, clinicians, and entrepreneurs driven by a shared purpose, to make healthcare more human through technology. Our founders bring deep experience in AI, healthcare innovation, and enterprise systems, and we've built a team that combines technical excellence with compassion and curiosity.

At Pythagoras AI, we move fast, think deeply, and care fiercely. We believe that building for healthcare means building with responsibility and precision. Every system we design, every workflow we automate, and every conversation we power has the potential to improve someone's life.

**Mission Statement**
Empowering healthcare providers with empathetic voice AI to make care safer, more accessible, and more human.

**Our Values**
* Patients first, Always. Safety, trust, and dignity come before everything.
* Empathy in design. We create AI that listens, understands, and cares.
* Quality and safety above all. Precision matters when lives are involved.
* Curiosity drives us. We question, learn, and innovate continuously.
* We grow together. Collaboration, ownership, and shared purpose define our culture.

**The Role**
We're hiring our first Member of Technical Staff to sit at the center of our customers, internal stakeholders, and engineering team. You'll work closely with product, sales, clinical operations, and customers to understand what healthcare providers actually need, shape how Clara behaves for each customer, and ship the changes that make Clara work in the real world.
This is not a support role and not a pure engineering role. You'll collaborate across the team and with customers as needed, write code, and have direct influence over what we build next.
