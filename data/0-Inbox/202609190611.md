
# What Employers Are Looking For

When companies ask "What interests you about working for us?", they're actually testing three things:

1. **Specific understanding** — Did you actually read the posting, or is this a generic "I love your mission" template? 
2. **Alignment** — Do your values match theirs? Do you *get* what makes them different?
3. **Why you** — Why would *you* be well-suited, not just any candidate?

❌ *"I have 25 years of experience across..."* (they don't care about breadth; they want ownership)
