
# What interests you about working for this company?

When companies ask "What interests you about working for us?", they're actually testing three things:

1. **Specific understanding** — Did you actually read the posting, or is this a generic "I love your mission" template?
2. **Alignment** — Do your values match theirs? Do you _get_ what makes them different?
3. **Why you** — Why would _you_ be well-suited, not just any candidate?
